Letting my chaotic mind lead the way through my artistic practice, my works become a physical manifestation of my mind.

Currently enraptured by the depths of fandom and the human nature to adore and obsess, my works illustrate this nature of the fan throughout time and expand on fandom beyond the digital perspective.
